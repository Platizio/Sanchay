# MVP package final critic

**Verdict.** NOT READY AS WRITTEN. The package is fixable, and most fixes are rewrites of the plan documents, not new design.

**What is sound:**
- All four owner scope groups trace to backend tasks (Plans 01–04).
- Every non-negotiable has an owning task and a test: consent-first with DB-recomputed JCS, exact decimal money with lossless-json, Idempotency-Key, worker-only FP calls, RECONCILING, webhook dedupe, auth and re-fetch, the OTP policy, PII redaction, RDS encryption and TLS, rate limiting, golden vectors, FakeFp plus sandbox smoke.
- Every deferred item has a phase-2 target, and new KYC is correctly the default deferral, extension E5.
- Brand and host rules largely hold.
- Holidays match the roadmap list (checked: 10-02, 10-20, Diwali leave 11-09/10, 11-24).

**Three structural defects must be fixed before S1 planning locks on Mon 09-28:**

1. **The capacity model is not credible and the documents disagree.**
   - The MVP quietly drops the roadmap's 0.75 focus factor, planned leave and most of the review and probe overhead.
   - The assumed 1.6 therefore equals about 2.1 on the roadmap basis, above the measured upside of 1.9.
   - Even on the MVP's own basis, bottom-up demand is about 86 d against 80.4 d. The break-even factor is about 1.75, or about 1.67 with T1–T6.
   - The spec and sprint plans say the plan fits; the delta sheet and outlines say it does not.

2. **S1/S2 content and the 10-09 and 10-23 milestones exist in four incompatible versions**, and old and new task ids collide (B18, and trim T6 vs the T6 lookup).

3. **The dev AWS stack has been pushed to S3/S4**, yet the sandbox webhooks and returns, the 10-23 callback-URL promise to Cybrilla and the 11-06 demo all depend on it.

**Consequence for the gate.** The Fri 11-27 real-money GO/NO-GO is achievable only if the following happen:
- the owner (not the PO) pre-authorises T1–T6 at 10-09, with T7/T8 as dated contingencies;
- dev AWS is protected in S2;
- S3 is reordered so a lumpsum demo is ready on 11-06, or an API-driven demo is agreed with Cybrilla;
- the gate is tiered: GO-1 covers onboarding, lumpsum and redemption on 11-27; SIP follows under GO-2 after its canary.

Production credentials by Mon 11-16 remain a zero-float external dependency. The NO-GO fallback (orders disabled, re-gate 12-04/12-11) is correctly specified and should be treated as a likely outcome unless S2 measures a factor of at least 1.67.

**Before any code or DLT filing, also close these major items:**
- the four-template SMS/DLT decision with counsel sign-off before 10-12, and a mandatory hash line;
- guard exemptions and ALB path routing (these determine `user_ip` correctness);
- readiness no longer depending on NAV age;
- consent OTP evidence retention;
- a sanctioned SIP-cancel path;
- the missing account/SYS-01/pilot-terms tasks;
- owner acknowledgement of the synchronous-OTP deviation.
